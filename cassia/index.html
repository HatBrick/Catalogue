Update - October 13, 2026

Gonna start posting updates on this page as little journal entries, just to organize my thoughts and have a sort or mental basecamp to come back to when I feel lost. I have a good deal of projects in the pipeline at the moment, just need to set some deadlines for when they need to be finished by so I can give myself some structure.

My newest EP, We Used to Make our Tyrants Flee should be released before the midterms on November 3, though ideally I can finish it a bit before then. I have the instrumentals recorded for 5 of the 6 songs, and just need to find a time to record the lyrics without making too much noise (curse my desire to write excusively grindcore).

The pokemon creepypasta hack I've had in the works for literal years is something else I want to keep on my own radar, it's been real easy to forget about, but I just need to dedicate like, two weeks to working excusively on that. October is kinda the perfect time to do that, maybe I can set a Halloween deadline for myself.

I've been trying to draw more as well, I do think I need more artistic community in my like, and drawing is the fastest thing to do for sure in terms of getting a finished product to show people. Unfortunately, I don't really have any drawing projects that are like, big milestones for me, outside of untitled furry ghost comic.

Writing wise, Hunters is something I'm really eager to continue, I just need to share the first few chapters with someone and get their feedback, both to know what I should do more of moving forward, and so I can get an extra push of motivation outside my own will to do things (which has been a bit unreliable as of recent). Book of REM is another writing project I need to get back into, especially since its structure is very good for quickly hopping into.

The only other thing to think about I suppose would be any games I want to work on with Sasha, though I have so much of my own work at the moment that needs to be prioritized. Oh also the Simpleflips iceberg video I want to make, I did finally start writing the script for that, but I should just make the image first. I suppose I can set myself some goal dates and key actions like they have me do at work lol. I'll stick to one thing at a time for now so I don't overwhelm myself.
