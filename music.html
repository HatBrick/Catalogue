My Music

I make music too!! I'm not exactly sure how to pin down the kind of music I make, but generally I make rock and metal, as well as some hip-hop influenced electronic, sampling and plunderphonics, and general indie and experimental noise. I also release under several names, mainly to deliniate the type of music I make. You can find all the stuff I've made below, along with links to my music accounts and ways to listen.

REM and The Raven (2019-2021, 2026-Present)

REM and The Raven is the name I release most of my music under. The concept of REM and The Raven has always been a collection of self-contained stories that make most sense to be told through song. Most of my music is instrumental, with story told through song titles, melody, and atmosphere. Most music here is guitar, drum machines, and piano, with some synths and sampling mixed in. Most of my music was recorded from late 2018 to late 2019 in sporadic bursts, later blending in more midi instruments, and a lot of it still unreleased. There's a decent amount of old stuff I want to rework into new releases, and Trukko will be receiving a volume two (eventually), but for the time being I want to focus on recording more guitar and futzing with garageband amps.

Mālak (2021-2026)

I decided to release this project under a new name as it didn't fit with anything I didn't feel right to include it under either previous name I had used. It made the most sense in my head to release it as a stand alone project (that decision was also helped by the fact that it is a completely unique sound to anything I've done before and honestly, probably anything I will do moving forward. מֲלְאָךְ (or mālak) from Hebrew. To reign, or inceptively, ascend to the throne. 4427.

Trukko Auburn (2019-2020)

A music dump account I originally made to post DAW beats I made. I continued releasing music under this name for 2020, mostly projects that I didn't feel had enough structure to be REM and The Raven material. Actually I think the main reason why I made this account is cause Sound Cloud only lets you release 300 minutes of music before you have to pay for premium, and I don't wanna do that lol. I may release stuff under this name in the future, but if I do it won't be to Sound Cloud cause I'm fully at my limit there. In any case, I'm not pumpin' out music like I used to, so there's not much reason for the moment to have a dump account.
