    • Well Done My Good and Faithful Servant - Mālak (2021-2026)

    I decided to release this project under a new name as it didn't fit with anything I didn't feel right to include it under either previous name I had used. It made the most sense in my head to release it as a stand alone project (that decision was also helped by the fact that it is a completely unique sound to anything I've done before and honestly, probably anything I will do moving forward. מֲלְאָךְ (or mālak) from Hebrew. To reign, or inceptively, ascend to the throne. 4427.

    What started as a NONE OF THIS IS REAL rip-off ened up being my longest running finished project by a long shot. Its direction and meaning shifted quite a lot over the course of its development, but I'm fairly proud of what it eventually became, even if I have mixed feelings on many of the older songs.

    The album is split into four parts, each representing a different part of the past half-decade before and during its creation. In some ways it's more of a vent piece than anything, angry, bitter, hateful of the Trump Administration and the ideology of Christian Nationalism. There's a lot of thought behind many of the songs and their specific connection to the world we live in now, but I'd feel tacky going through them one by one, meaning is better left unspoken.

    The album is a mixture of field recordings, drum & bass, synth and electronic beats, atmospheric and harsh noise, and generally experimental shit I cooked up at 2am. This album is very much made for me and my interests, I highly doubt there's another person on the planet who will enjoy this project as a whole, but I suppose you make art for yourself anyways. If other people enjoy it then that's just a bonus.

    Calypso - REM and The Raven (2021)

    REM and The Raven is the name I release most of my music under. The concept of REM and The Raven has always been a collection of self-contained stories that make most sense to be told through song. Most of my music is instrumental, with story told through song titles, melody, and atmosphere. Most music here is guitar, drum machines, and piano, with some synths and sampling mixed in. Most of my music was recorded from late 2018 to late 2019 in sporadic bursts, later blending in more midi instruments, and a lot of it still unreleased. There's a decent amount of old stuff I want to rework into new releases, and Trukko will be receiving a volume two (eventually), but for the time being I want to focus on recording more guitar and fucking with garageband amps.

    Trukko - REM and The Raven (2019)

    What I consider to be my first proper album, much more fleshed out and distinct than Cherryville. The first song I recorded for this album was Wolfman, inspired pretty heavily by Dance Gavin Dance's Self-Trepanation and The Fall of Troy's Macaulay McCulkin. I developed the album's story as I continued working on it, mostly taking from insecurities I was dealing with at the time regarding my identity and self-acceptance.

    Cherryville - REM and The Raven (2018)

    The first music I ever recorded, very directly inspired by sabella's DOG DAZE. The week after I first heard that album, I was inspired to try and create something similar, it sounded like I could do it. This album was recorded in two days, and as such it is fairly simple. I really only had three distinct song ideas initially, every song with the exception of two were recorded in one take, and there is very little polish if any. I'm glad I made it though, it taught me that I could make things, and I recorded a pretty good amount over the next year.
