Current Project

The following catalogue is an attempt to put in one place the things I feel I must do in my lifetime. This doesn't necissarily encapsulate every little thing I want to write or code or produce, but it does reflect the things I would most regret not finishing at the end of my life.

Major Triumps

Because I am an insane person and have played wayyy too much CIV VII recently, I am seperating this list into major and minor triumps, major triumps being something that is to me a necessity that I create, and minor triumphs being things that are more "just for the fun of it". The real thing that seperates these two is how long this idea has stuck around for, and how big of an idea it is. For better or for worse (definitely for worse), the bigger the project is, the more important it will feel to me. So there are a lot of things here I care an awful lot about, but some may simply be too small of a project for me to consider particularly lofty of a goal.

Book of REM

It was around high school that I came up with the idea for REM. Given that this is the first item on the list (and that I named by band after it), obviously this idea is very near and dear to me. This section serves twofold however. This work is a piece of writing I have started and would like to finish, an expansive, convoluted, and cultish book of fables, dialogues, philosophy, cosmology, sheet music, and poetry. I will probably share snippets here and there on this site, but I want to retain a confusion surrounding the book that this sort of explination would not serve.

The other meaning of REM however, has more to do with the general universe in which most of my work resides. Many of my stories take place within this shared universe
