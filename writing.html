My Writing

I've been writing since like, 2016? 2015?? Jesus. Most of that stuff was kinda crap but it got me into the concept of writing seriously, as I've always had ideas for stories and shows, and I really wanted to do something with them. I don't know if I'd still consider writing my main thing, but it's truly what I am most interested in doing with my life, even if most of the stories I am most desperate to tell are the ones I have yet to begin.

My writing mostly consists of short horror and fantasy. I have written a few novellas, but most of my finished work is very old (pre 2020). Most of my more recent work are in-progress, longer pieces.
